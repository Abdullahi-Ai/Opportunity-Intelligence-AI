### Program Overview
The Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA) invites applications from continuing students and fresh graduates for its competitive attachment and internship programs. Trainees gain direct experience in revenue administration, tax audit, customs border operations, legal enforcement, data analytics, and enterprise cybersecurity.

### Eligibility Criteria
- **For 3-Month Attachment:** Must be a continuing 2nd, 3rd, or 4th-year student in a recognized university or college with a formal requirement from the institution for industrial attachment.
- **For 12-Month Internship:** Must have graduated with a Bachelor's Degree or Diploma within the last 24 months, with no prior formal employment or participation in KRA internship.
- Minimum cumulative academic performance: Upper Second-Class Honours or equivalent.
- Age: Between 20 and 30 years old.
- Must be a Kenyan citizen with clean background checks.

### Required Documents
- National Identification Card (ID).
- For Attachment: Formal introductory letter from the university Dean or Industrial Attachment Coordinator.
- For Internship: Official Degree/Diploma Certificate and complete Transcripts.
- Valid KRA PIN Certificate.
- Valid NHIF / Social Health Authority (SHA) medical insurance cover.
- Comprehensive Curriculum Vitae (CV).

### Application Steps & Selection
1. Access the KRA e-Recruitment Portal: https://erecruitment.kra.go.ke.
2. Register as a new applicant or sign in with your credentials.
3. Complete your personal profile and upload scanned certified copies of your certificates, transcripts, and institution letter.
4. Apply specifically for either "Undergraduate Attachment Intake" or "Graduate Internship Intake".
5. Shortlisted candidates are contacted via the official KRA email domain (@kra.go.ke) for written aptitude tests and background security vetting.
