### Program Overview
The Public Service Internship Programme (PSIP) is a national initiative by the Government of Kenya through the Public Service Commission to provide unemployed degree holders with a 12-month hands-on apprenticeship in public sector ministries, state departments, and government agencies. Interns receive a monthly consolidated stipend prescribed by the Salaries and Remuneration Commission (SRC).

### Eligibility Criteria
- Must be a Kenyan citizen.
- Must hold a recognized Bachelor's degree in any discipline from an accredited university.
- Must have graduated not earlier than 2023 (must not have had prior public service employment).
- Must NOT have previously served in the PSIP internship program.
- Must be computer literate and possess sound interpersonal communication skills.

### Required Documents
- Scanned copy of Bachelor's Degree Certificate or provisional graduation completion letter.
- Official certified academic transcripts for all 4 (or 5) years of study.
- Copy of Kenyan National Identity Card.
- Copy of valid KRA PIN Certificate.
- Curriculum Vitae (CV) detailing education and community leadership.
- Note: Post-selection clearance requires a Certificate of Good Conduct (DCI) and personal medical insurance cover.

### Application Steps & Selection
1. Visit the Public Service Commission jobs portal: https://www.publicservice.go.ke or https://psckjobs.go.ke.
2. Create or log into your PSC applicant profile.
3. Complete the personal background, educational qualifications, and county of residence sections.
4. Select the "PSIP Internship Advertisement" under the open vacancies tab and submit.
5. PSC validates applications against regional and gender equity quotas, followed by verification of academic certificates.
