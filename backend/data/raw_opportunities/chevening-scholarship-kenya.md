### Program Overview
Chevening is the UK government’s international awards programme aimed at developing global leaders. Funded by the Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Office (FCDO), Chevening offers full financial support for individuals to study for any eligible one-year master’s degree at any UK university while gaining access to an influential global alumni network.

### Eligibility Criteria
- Be a citizen of Kenya and commit to return to Kenya for a minimum of two years after your award has ended.
- Have completed an undergraduate degree that will enable you to gain entry into a postgraduate programme at a UK university (typically equivalent to an upper second-class 2:1 honours degree in the UK).
- Have at least two years of work experience (equivalent to 2,800 hours), which may include full-time, part-time, voluntary, or paid internships.
- Apply to three different eligible UK university courses and receive an unconditional offer from one of these choices by the UK university deadline.

### Required Documents
- Official University Degree Certificate and complete Academic Transcripts.
- Two professional and/or academic references in official letterhead format.
- Four distinct 500-word essays: Leadership & Influence, Relationship Building & Networking, Study in the UK Motivation, and Career Plan.
- Valid Kenyan Passport.
- Unconditional offers from chosen UK university master's programmes (required before final award interview).

### Application Steps & Selection
1. Submit your application online via the Chevening portal: https://www.chevening.org/scholarship/kenya/.
2. Complete all eligibility questions and upload your 4 core essays.
3. Applications are sifted by independent reading committees against leadership criteria.
4. Shortlisted applicants are invited for an interview at the British High Commission in Nairobi between February and April.
5. Final awards are announced in June, with studies commencing in September/October in the UK.
