### Program Overview
The Presidential Digital Talent Programme (PDTP / Digika) is a public-private partnership coordinated by the ICT Authority. It develops high-caliber digital talent for the public and private sectors through specialized technical training, industry mentorship, deployment on national digital transformation projects, and funding for internationally recognized certifications (e.g., AWS, Cisco, Microsoft, CEH).

### Eligibility Criteria
- Must be a citizen of Kenya.
- Must have graduated with a Bachelor's Degree in an ICT or related Engineering discipline within the last 24 months.
- Must have attained a First Class Honours or Upper Second-Class Honours (2:1).
- Unemployed at the time of application and able to commit full-time for 12 continuous months.
- Under 29 years of age at the time of application.

### Required Documents
- Valid Kenyan National Identity Card.
- Bachelor's Degree Certificate and official Academic Transcripts.
- KCSE Certificate or official result slip.
- Curriculum Vitae (CV) with relevant project experience and technical repositories.
- Valid Certificate of Good Conduct from the Directorate of Criminal Investigations (DCI) prior to contract signing.

### Application Steps & Selection
1. Register on the official Digika portal: https://digitalent.go.ke.
2. Complete your personal bio, academic records, and upload scanned academic certificates.
3. Complete the online aptitude and technical screening assessment covering software, networks, and general logic.
4. Top-ranked candidates across all 47 counties are invited for physical or regional oral interviews.
5. Inducted interns receive a monthly stipend, private sector rotation (e.g., Safaricom, Microsoft ADC, Huawei, Cisco), and fully funded international IT certification exams.
