### Program Overview
The Safaricom Discover Graduate Management Trainee Programme is an immersive 18-month talent development pipeline. Trainees rotate across core divisions including Enterprise Business, Financial Services (M-PESA), Technology, Consumer Business, and Big Data / AI, working alongside executive mentors on production products.

### Eligibility Criteria
- Completed an undergraduate degree within the last 12 to 24 months, or awaiting graduation with completion verification.
- Minimum academic achievement of Upper Second-Class Honours (2:1) or GPA of 3.3/4.0 and above.
- Minimum Kenya Certificate of Secondary Education (KCSE) mean grade of B+ (or equivalent) with strong performance in Mathematics and English.
- Maximum age of 26 years at the time of application.
- Demonstrable extracurricular leadership, digital fluency, curiosity, and high agility.

### Required Documents
- Comprehensive Curriculum Vitae (CV) highlighting projects, internships, and technical skills.
- Certified Bachelor's Degree Certificate or provisional university letter of completion.
- Official certified academic transcripts for all years of undergraduate study.
- Copy of Kenyan National ID card or Passport.
- Portfolio or GitHub link (encouraged for engineering, software, and data science applicants).

### Application Steps & Selection
1. Apply via the Safaricom Careers portal: https://www.safaricom.co.ke/careers.
2. Complete online psychometric, numerical, and inductive reasoning assessments.
3. For technical applicants, participate in a live coding or technical challenge on HackerRank/Codility.
4. Attend an intensive virtual or in-person Assessment Centre involving business case studies, group problem-solving, and executive panel presentations.
5. Offers extended with full employee compensation package, medical benefits, and structured leadership coaching.
