### Program Overview
The Hult Prize challenges student teams from universities across Kenya to solve a pressing social challenge aligned with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs). Teams ideate, prototype, and pitch scalable social enterprises. Campus winners advance to Regional Summits (Nairobi, Dubai, Lisbon, etc.) and the Global Accelerator in London.

### Eligibility Criteria
- Must be a currently enrolled student at an accredited Kenyan university or college.
- Must form a team of 3 to 5 students from the same university institution.
- Must propose a for-profit or hybrid business model with measurable positive social or environmental impact.
- Cannot submit an idea previously funded by more than $50,000 in external venture capital.
- Must be able to present pitch decks and prototypes in English.

### Required Documents
- Proof of active student enrollment (Student ID or current semester fee receipt).
- 10-slide Pitch Deck outlining Problem, Solution, Market Opportunity, Business Model, and SDG alignment.
- 2-minute Video Elevator Pitch introducing team members and value proposition.
- Executive summary of the social enterprise venture.

### Application Steps & Selection
1. Register your team on the official Hult Prize On-Campus portal for your university (e.g., UoN, Strathmore, JKUAT, Kenyatta University) at https://www.hultprize.org.
2. Attend on-campus mentorship and design thinking bootcamps.
3. Compete in the On-Campus Finals judged by leading Kenyan venture capitalists and social impact founders.
4. On-campus winning teams receive direct qualification and travel sponsorship for international Regional Summits.
