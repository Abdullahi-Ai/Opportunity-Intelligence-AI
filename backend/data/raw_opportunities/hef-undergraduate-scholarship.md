### Program Overview
The Higher Education Financing (HEF) framework is the official Kenyan government model providing need-based scholarships, tuition loans, and maintenance allowances to students joining or enrolled in universities and TVET colleges. Funding is allocated based on Means Testing Instrument (MTI) bands ranging from Band 1 (Vulnerable) to Band 5 (Least Needy).

### Eligibility Criteria
- Must be a Kenyan citizen with a valid National Identity Card (or birth certificate if under 18 with parent ID).
- Must have sat for the Kenya Certificate of Secondary Education (KCSE) and been placed by KUCCPS in an accredited university or TVET institution.
- Must have a valid Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA) Personal Identification Number (PIN).
- Valid email address and a personal phone number registered under the applicant's name.
- Continuous assessment: continuing students must maintain active academic registration.

### Required Documents
- Valid Applicant National ID or Waiting Card.
- Applicant KRA PIN Certificate.
- KCSE Result Slip or Certificate.
- KUCCPS Admission Letter with official Student Registration Number.
- Parents/Guardians National ID numbers, death certificates (if deceased), or proof of vulnerability.
- Two Guarantors' National IDs and valid phone numbers (for the loan component).
- Valid student bank account details for maintenance allowance disbursement.

### Application Steps & Selection
1. Create a student account on the official portal: https://www.hef.co.ke.
2. Complete the profile information including secondary school history, family background, and socioeconomic data.
3. Upload clear scanned copies of all required identification and admission documents.
4. Submit the application for MTI classification and wait for official SMS/email notification of the funding band award.
5. Sign the loan agreement form online through the portal or the HELB mobile app.

### Deadlines & Verification
- Annual cycle opens in June and closes on December 31st for the current academic year.
- Note: Always verify disbursement schedules directly with your university finance dean or through the official HEF student portal.
