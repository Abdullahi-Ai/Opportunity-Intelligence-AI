### Program Overview
The Equity Leaders Program (ELP) is the flagship leadership development initiative of Equity Group Foundation. It selects top academic performers in the Kenya Certificate of Secondary Education (top boy and top girl in every sub-county where Equity operates, plus Wings to Fly scholars scoring A and A-). Scholars undergo a paid internship at Equity Bank branches across Kenya and receive comprehensive college preparation support for world-class global universities (Harvard, MIT, Cambridge, Ashesi, etc.).

### Eligibility Criteria
- Top male and top female academic performers in the Kenya Certificate of Secondary Education (KCSE) in each administrative sub-county.
- Wings to Fly alumni who attained an A (plain) or A- (minus) in the recent KCSE examination.
- Demonstrated integrity, high character, and commitment to transformative leadership in Kenya.
- Open to students awaiting university placement or currently transitioning to higher education.

### Required Documents
- Certified copy of KCSE Result Slip / High School Leaving Certificate.
- Copy of Kenyan National ID card or birth certificate.
- Wings to Fly scholarship completion certificate (if applicable).
- Recommendation letter from Secondary School Principal.
- Character reference letter from local administration (Chief or Assistant County Commissioner).

### Application Steps & Selection
1. Applications are coordinated through local Equity Bank branches nationwide immediately following the official release of KCSE results.
2. Visit your nearest Equity Bank branch or consult the Foundation portal: https://equitygroupfoundation.com.
3. Complete the physical/digital ELP intake forms with certified copies of your KCSE results and school endorsements.
4. Shortlisted candidates undergo a rigorous panel interview with the District/Branch Scholarship Selection Board (BSSB).
5. Inducted scholars begin their paid branch internship, earning a monthly stipend and receiving mentorship from senior banking executives.
