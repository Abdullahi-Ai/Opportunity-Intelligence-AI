### Program Overview
Safaricom PLC offers a 3-month structured Industrial Attachment Programme designed to give continuing Kenyan university and diploma students practical, hands-on experience in telecommunications, software engineering, digital IT, network operations, finance, and enterprise business solutions.

### Eligibility Criteria
- Must be an enrolled, continuing student in an accredited Kenyan university or college.
- Must have a mandatory requirement from your academic institution to undertake industrial attachment for a minimum of 8 to 12 weeks.
- Minimum cumulative academic performance equivalent to Second Class Upper Division (or GPA of 3.0/4.0 or 60%+ average).
- Must have completed at least 2 full years of university study (or 1 year for Diploma candidates).
- Must possess strong problem-solving skills, basic coding/networking skills (for technical tracks), and high integrity.

### Required Documents
- Valid Student ID Card.
- Official Industrial Attachment introductory letter from the Dean of Students or Head of Department.
- Comprehensive Curriculum Vitae (CV) / Resume (maximum 2 pages).
- Valid National Hospital Insurance Fund (NHIF) / Social Health Insurance (SHA) cover or personal accident insurance.
- Copy of National Identity Card.
- Official University Academic Transcripts covering completed academic years.

### Application Steps & Selection
1. Visit the Safaricom Careers Portal at https://www.safaricom.co.ke/careers.
2. Register an applicant profile on the career engine.
3. Search for "Industrial Attachment Intake" and submit your CV, cover letter, and institution introduction letter.
4. Shortlisted candidates complete an automated online assessment followed by a phone/virtual interview with the department leads.
5. Successful applicants receive a formal attachment contract and workplace orientation in Nairobi or designated regional hubs.
