### Program Overview
The Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program at United States International University-Africa (USIU-Africa) provides comprehensive scholarships to talented youth facing severe socioeconomic barriers. The scholarship covers 100% tuition, mandatory fees, housing, meals, books, health insurance, a laptop, and monthly living stipends.

### Eligibility Criteria
- Must be 29 years old or younger at the time of application.
- Must have completed high school (KCSE in Kenya or equivalent) with a minimum mean grade of B (plain) or higher.
- Must demonstrate academic talent, a commitment to giving back to their community, and clear leadership potential.
- Must demonstrate significant financial need that would otherwise prevent university attendance.
- Open to citizens of African nations, with dedicated quotas for young women, refugees, displaced persons, and students living with disabilities.

### Required Documents
- Certified copies of High School Certificate / KCSE Result Slip.
- Certified copy of National ID, Passport, or UNHCR Refugee Mandate Certificate.
- Two completed letters of recommendation (one from high school principal/teacher, one from community leader/religious leader).
- Completed financial verification section with utility receipts, death certificates (if orphaned), or social welfare letters.
- Personal essays detailing leadership experience, community engagement, and future vision.

### Application Steps & Selection
1. Download or access the official online application via the USIU-Africa portal: https://www.usiu.ac.ke/mastercard-foundation-scholars-program/.
2. Fill all personal, academic, and financial background sections accurately.
3. Attach certified supporting documents and essays.
4. Shortlisted applicants will be invited for comprehensive interviews and leadership assessments in Nairobi or virtually.
5. Final admission is contingent on formal acceptance into USIU-Africa undergraduate programs.
