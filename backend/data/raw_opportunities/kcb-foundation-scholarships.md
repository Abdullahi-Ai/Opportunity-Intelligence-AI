### Program Overview
The KCB Foundation Tertiary and 2jiajiri Scholarship Programme funds education and practical skills development for youth across Kenya. The program provides full and partial tuition scholarships, toolkits for enterprise development, and targeted financial support with a minimum 25% allocation dedicated to youth living with disabilities.

### Eligibility Criteria
- Must be a Kenyan citizen.
- Demonstrated financial need (orphaned, vulnerable, or from low-income family backgrounds).
- Completed secondary school with qualifications for tertiary, TVET, or undergraduate entry.
- Minimum of 25% of scholarships reserved for persons with disabilities (disability card from NCPWD required).
- Passion for enterprise creation or practical skills acquisition.

### Required Documents
- Scanned copy of National ID Card.
- KCSE Result Slip / School Leaving Certificate.
- Admission letter from an accredited tertiary institution, university, or TVET centre in Kenya.
- Letter of endorsement from Area Chief, local religious leader, or village elder confirming financial vulnerability.
- National Council for Persons with Disabilities (NCPWD) registration certificate (if applying under the disability quota).

### Application Steps & Selection
1. Collect physical application forms from any KCB Bank branch nationwide or apply online at https://kcbgroup.com/foundation.
2. Fill all details regarding your household income, chosen course, and institution.
3. Attach certified copies of all verification letters and certificates.
4. Interviews are conducted by County Selection Panels at designated regional KCB branches.
5. Successful candidates have their tuition fees disbursed directly to their training institutions.
