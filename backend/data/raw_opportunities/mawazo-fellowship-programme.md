### Program Overview
The Mawazo Learning Exchange (MLEx) Fellowship is a one-year virtual and residential fellowship for early-career African women pursuing PhD studies. Headquartered in Nairobi, Kenya, the Mawazo Institute equips scholars with research methodology masterclasses, science communication skills, policy engagement training, research grant funding, and conference travel support.

### Eligibility Criteria
- Must be a woman citizen of an African country.
- Must be currently enrolled in a PhD programme at an accredited university within Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, or Rwanda.
- Research focus must address pressing local or global development challenges (e.g., climate change, sustainable agriculture, public health, emerging technologies).
- Must have completed your PhD coursework (if applicable) and had your research proposal formally approved by your department.
- Able to commit 3-5 hours per week to online fellowship modules and attend physical convenings in Nairobi.

### Required Documents
- Proof of official enrollment in a recognized PhD program in Kenya or East Africa.
- Formal university letter confirming approval of your PhD research proposal.
- 5-page Research Summary detailing problem statement, methodology, expected impact, and timeline.
- Curriculum Vitae (CV) emphasizing academic publications and research contributions.
- One letter of recommendation from your primary PhD Academic Supervisor.

### Application Steps & Selection
1. Complete the online application via the Mawazo portal: https://mawazoinstitute.org.
2. Upload your supervisor's reference letter and research proposal summary.
3. Submissions are judged by a panel of African researchers and disciplinary specialists.
4. Top-scoring candidates are invited for a virtual 30-minute interview and presentation.
5. Selected fellows receive research micro-grants, publication support, and travel grants.
