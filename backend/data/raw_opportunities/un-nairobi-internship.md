### Program Overview
The United Nations Office at Nairobi (UNON) hosts the global headquarters of the UN Environment Programme (UNEP) and UN-Habitat. The internship programme offers students practical experience in multilateral diplomacy, environmental policy, urban resilience, climate science communication, and international project monitoring.

### Eligibility Criteria
- Must be enrolled in a Master's or PhD programme, OR in the final year of a first university degree (Bachelor's or equivalent).
- If graduated with a Bachelor's, must commence the internship within one year of graduation.
- Fluency in English (oral and written) is required. Working knowledge of French or other UN official languages is an added advantage.
- High proficiency in computer applications, research methodology, and drafting policy briefs.
- Must not have immediate family members employed within the UN Secretariat.

### Required Documents
- Scanned copy of Student ID or proof of university enrollment / graduation certificate.
- Certified copy of Academic Transcripts.
- Customized Cover Letter detailing motivation for UNEP/UN-Habitat and specific skills.
- Completed UN Inspira Personal History Profile (PHP).
- Valid medical insurance covering emergency care and hospitalization in Kenya.

### Application Steps & Selection
1. Create a profile on the UN Careers Inspira portal: https://careers.un.org.
2. Search for "Internship - Nairobi" and filter by duty station "Nairobi".
3. Draft a tailored PHP highlighting relevant coursework, volunteer experience, and extracurricular initiatives.
4. Shortlisted candidates are invited for a written assessment (e.g., policy drafting exercise) followed by a competency-based interview via Microsoft Teams.
5. Successful applicants receive formal internship offer letters and security clearance for the UN Gigiri complex.
