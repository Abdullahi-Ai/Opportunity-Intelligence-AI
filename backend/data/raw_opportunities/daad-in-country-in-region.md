### Program Overview
The German Academic Exchange Service (DAAD) In-Country/In-Region programme aims at fostering strong, internationally oriented higher education systems in Sub-Saharan Africa. The scholarships fund Master's and PhD degrees at designated centre of excellence universities in Kenya (e.g., University of Nairobi, JKUAT, Moi University, ICIPE).

### Eligibility Criteria
- Must be a national of a Sub-Saharan African country.
- Must have completed your last university degree with above average results (at least Upper Second-Class Honours or equivalent).
- Your last degree must not have been awarded more than 6 years before the application deadline.
- Must be planning to pursue full-time Master's or Doctoral studies at an approved partner institution in Kenya.
- Female candidates, candidates from less privileged regions or vulnerable groups are strongly encouraged to apply.

### Required Documents
- Fully completed DAAD application form from the portal.
- Curriculum Vitae in Europass format (free from https://europa.eu).
- Certified copies of university degree certificates and complete academic transcripts.
- For Master's: A 2-page Statement of Motivation.
- For PhD: A detailed Research Proposal (max 10 pages) with an explicit timetable and confirmation of academic supervision.
- Two recent recommendation letters from senior academic professors.

### Application Steps & Selection
1. Step 1: Apply for admission at the designated host university or institute in Kenya (e.g., UoN, JKUAT, ICIPE).
2. Step 2: Concurrently register and submit your scholarship application on the DAAD portal: https://www.daad.or.ke.
3. Applications are reviewed by an independent multinational selection committee composed of African and German university professors.
4. Award notifications are communicated directly through the DAAD portal, covering tuition fees, medical insurance, research grants, and monthly living allowances.
